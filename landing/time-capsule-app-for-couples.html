Couples

A time capsule app for couples.

Not every couple lives in the same city, or even the same time zone. Capsule lets you both drop photos into one private album as you go, then unlock it together, on your terms: an anniversary, a visit, or the day you're finally in the same room.

Last updated: July 24, 2026

A time capsule app for couples is a private shared album that the two of you add to over weeks or months, sealed until a date you both agreed on. Neither person can look early (not even whoever created it), so the album stays genuinely unseen until it opens for both of you at once.

It works because couples already take photos constantly and almost never look at them again. Sealing them changes what you put in: instead of the trip highlights you'd post anywhere, you start saving the Tuesday-night things, because you know the version of you opening it in a year won't remember them.

Why couples

Built for the distance, not around it.

Long-distance couples and military families already know the ache of a missed moment. A shared, time-locked album turns waiting into anticipation instead: something you're both looking forward to, not just getting through.

  • – Both partners add photos privately, from wherever you each are
  • – Set an unlock date: a specific anniversary, or a date you'll finally be together
  • – Proximity unlock: the capsule can open the instant you're physically reunited, no date required
  • – Nothing to peek at early: the anticipation is part of the point
How it works

How do couples use a time capsule?

01

Pick the date you’ll open it

An anniversary, a lease ending, the day a long-distance stretch closes. The date is the whole design decision. Everything else follows from how far away it is.

02

Add things as they happen

Photos and short videos, dropped in over months. Nothing is visible to either of you, which is what stops it turning into a feed you both idly scroll.

03

Open it in the same room, or not

It unlocks for both of you simultaneously wherever you are. You can also set it to open only once you’re physically together, which is useful if the whole point is the reunion.

What should go in a couple’s time capsule?

The trap is filling it with the same photos you'd have taken anyway. The ordinary material is what actually lands in a year.

  • – The apartment as it currently is, genuinely, before you move and forget it
  • – A short video of each of you answering the same question, recorded months apart
  • – The bad photos: the ones where someone is mid-sentence and the light is wrong
  • – Screenshots of the conversation that made you laugh at 1am
  • – Whatever you were cooking on an unremarkable weeknight
  • – The view out of each of your windows, if you’re in different cities
  • – Tickets, receipts, handwriting: photographed rather than described
  • – A message recorded for the two of you who will open it

    • Long-distance couples get the most out of this, because the two halves of the album are visibly different places, and neither of you has seen the other half.

How long should a couple’s time capsule stay locked?

Three months is the shortest window that still surprises you. Under that, you remember everything you put in and the reveal falls flat.

One year is the version most couples want: long enough that the early entries genuinely surprise you, and it maps naturally onto an anniversary.

Until a specific event works better than a duration when there's a real date coming: the end of a deployment, a graduation, the month you finally share an address. Anticipation is easier to sustain when it's attached to something you're already counting down to.

If you're long distance, consider unlocking on physical proximity instead of a date: the capsule stays sealed until you're both actually in the same place, which makes the reunion itself the trigger.

Common questions

Does this work if it’s just the two of us?

Yes, two people is the most common shape. Nothing in a capsule depends on having a crowd, and with two contributors you both end up seeing roughly half an album you have never laid eyes on.

We’re long distance. Is that better or worse?

Better, honestly. The album ends up split between two cities neither of you was in, which is exactly the material that doesn’t survive a normal camera roll. Setting the unlock to trigger when you’re physically together turns the reunion into the reveal.

Can one of us peek?

No. Surprise mode locks out the person who created the capsule as well, and it can’t be switched off afterwards. Whoever set it up is in exactly the same position as the other person.

What happens to the photos if we break up?

Deleting your account removes your own contributions, and capsules where you were the only member go with them. Nothing is published anywhere or shown to anyone outside the capsule at any point.
